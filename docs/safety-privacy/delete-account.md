# Can I delete my account at any time?

Yes. Go to **Settings > Account** and request account deletion. You'll need to type "DELETE MY ACCOUNT" to confirm.

After requesting deletion, there is a **30-day grace period** before your account and all associated data are permanently removed. This is required by law (GDPR) to give you time to change your mind.

**Important:** If you log back in during the 30-day period, the deletion is cancelled and the timer resets. To go through with the deletion, simply don't log in for 30 days after requesting it.
