# How does onboarding work?

When you first sign up for JustCollabs, you'll be guided through a **multi-step onboarding flow** that helps you set up your profile and get discovered by other creators.

## Step 1: Basics

- Enter your **nickname** (stage name or alias)
- Set your **date of birth** (you must be of legal age in the jurisdiction you live in)
- Choose your **gender**
- Select your **home base city** and country

## Step 2: Collaboration & Roles

- Select your **roles** on the platform (Performer, Photographer, Director, Editor, etc.)
- If you're a performer, you can fill in additional details like preferences and physical attributes

## Step 3: Profile Photo & Bio

- Upload a **profile photo** (SFW only) using the built-in crop tool
- Write a **short bio** to introduce yourself and describe what you're looking for

## Step 4: Social Connect

- Link your **Twitter (X)** or **Bluesky** account
- Add your **content platform URL** (OnlyFans, Fansly, etc.)
- These links help with verification and let others learn more about your work

## After Onboarding

Once onboarding is complete, you can always update your profile information from **Settings > Profile**. You'll also want to:

- **Apply for verification** to unlock full discovery features
- **Add travel plans** in Settings > Trips to show where you'll be
- **Browse the Performers page** to discover other creators (once verified)

The onboarding process takes just a few minutes and ensures your profile has everything it needs to attract the right collaborators.
