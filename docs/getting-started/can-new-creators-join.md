# Can I join if I'm new to content creation?

Yes, you can join JustCollabs even if you're relatively new. The key requirement is that you consistently publish content on social media — this shows us you're serious about adult content creation. As long as you are of legal age and can demonstrate an active presence on platforms like Twitter (X) or Bluesky, you are welcome to join and start building your network.
