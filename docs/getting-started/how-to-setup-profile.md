# How do I set up my profile?

When you first sign up, you'll be guided through a [multi-step onboarding flow](/getting-started/how-onboarding-works) that walks you through the essentials. After onboarding, you can fine-tune everything from your profile settings.

## During Onboarding

The onboarding flow covers the basics:
- **Nickname**: Your stage name or alias
- **Date of birth**: Age verification (must be 18+)
- **Gender & location**: Your gender and home base city
- **Roles**: What you do (performer, photographer, director, etc.)
- **Profile photo & bio**: Upload a photo using the crop tool and write your bio
- **Social links**: Connect Twitter (X) or Bluesky and add your content platform URL

## After Onboarding

You can update and expand your profile anytime from **Settings > Profile**. Here you can:

- **Update your avatar** using the built-in crop tool
- **Edit your bio** and personal details
- **Add or change your content platform URLs** (OnlyFans, Fansly, etc.)
- **Update social media links** (Twitter/X, Bluesky)
- **Fill in performer details** if applicable (experience level, preferences, physical attributes)
- **Set your home base city** for location-based discovery

## Other Settings

- **Settings > Trips**: Add travel plans to show where you'll be visiting
- **Settings > Preferences**: Adjust your discovery and notification preferences
- **Settings > Account**: Update your email or password

A complete and accurate profile increases your chances of getting verified and finding suitable collaborators.
