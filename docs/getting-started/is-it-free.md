# Is it free to use?

**Yes — and if you get verified today, it's free forever.** All features on JustCollabs are completely free to use for verified members. In the future, we may introduce optional premium services for new users, but anyone who is verified during the current early access period will never be charged or have to pay anything for using JustCollabs. This is our way of saying thank you for supporting the platform early on.
