# How do invites and rewards work?

Once you are verified on JustCollabs you get a personal invite link you can share with other creators. Inviting people helps grow the network and earns you profile badges.

## Getting your invite link

Go to **Settings → Invite** to find your invite link. It looks like this:

```
justcollabs.com/invite/{your-code}
```

Tap **Share** to send it via X (Twitter), Bluesky, WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS, or copy it to your clipboard. You have up to 100 invites available.

## How invites work

When someone signs up through your link, they are linked to your account as one of your invitees. Once they complete verification on JustCollabs, they count toward your badge progress.

Your **Invites** page shows everyone you've invited along with their current status: Verified, Pending, Denied, or Not Verified.

## Rewards and badges

Earn badges that appear on your public profile by bringing in verified creators:

### Founding Connector
Invite **3 creators who get verified** during the founding period. This badge is only available while the founding period is active — act early.

### Top Connector
Invite **10 creators who get verified** at any time. This badge also gives you **priority placement** in the creator discovery feed.

Once you earn Top Connector, it replaces Founding Connector on your profile.
