# How do DM requests work?

DM requests are how you connect with other creators on JustCollabs. They ensure both parties agree to chat before any conversation starts.

## How It Works

1. **Send a DM request** to a creator you'd like to connect with — include a short introduction message and verify your identity with Twitter (X) or Bluesky
2. **The creator reviews your request** in their **Requests** tab and decides whether to accept or decline
3. **If accepted**, you can both chat freely through the **Messages** tab

## Who Can Send DM Requests?

Only **verified** users can send DM requests. Unverified users cannot initiate DM requests, but they can receive and accept them. If a verified user reaches out to you first, you can chat with them regardless of your verification status.

## Can I Send DM Requests Without an Account?

Yes — you can send a DM request as a guest by visiting a creator's public profile at `justcollabs.com/{nickname}`. You'll need to verify your identity with Twitter or Bluesky. If the creator accepts, you'll be prompted to create an account to start chatting.
