# How do I change my preferences settings?

Go to **Settings > Preferences** to customize how JustCollabs displays information and how your profile appears to others.

## Unit Preferences

You can choose your preferred units for measurements displayed across the platform:

- **Distance** — Kilometers (km) or Miles (mi). Used when displaying distances between locations and nearby creators.
- **Weight** — Kilograms (kg) or Pounds (lb). Used on your profile and when filtering performers.
- **Height** — Centimeters (cm) or Feet/Inches (ft). Used on your profile and when filtering performers.

Changes are saved automatically when you select a new option.

## Hide My Age

Toggle **Hide my age** to prevent your age from being visible on your profile. Your date of birth is still stored for verification purposes, but other users won't see your age.

## Allow Homepage Featuring

The **Allow homepage featuring** toggle controls whether your profile can appear on the JustCollabs homepage. This is enabled by default and helps you get discovered by other creators. Turn it off if you prefer not to be featured.

## How do these settings affect other features?

Your unit preferences carry over to other parts of the platform. For example, when you use filters on the Performers page, height and weight ranges will display in whichever units you've selected here.
