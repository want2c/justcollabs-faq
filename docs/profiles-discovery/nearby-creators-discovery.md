# How do I find creators in nearby cities?

Finding creators in nearby cities is easy with JustCollabs' **distance discovery feature**! This helps you connect with creators you might be able to meet without traveling far.

## How It Works

1. **Open the Performers page** from the bottom navigation bar
2. **Select a city** using the location picker — choose your home city or any travel destination
3. **Browse nearby results** — creators in surrounding areas will appear with distance indicators
4. **Distance badges** show exactly how far each creator is from your selected city

## Visual Indicators

Each creator card shows:
- **Distance badge**: Small gray badge showing "25km", "45km", etc.
- **Location info**: Their home city or current travel location
- **Travel status**: Whether they're based there or visiting

## Strategic Benefits

- Find creators within driving distance for easy meetups
- Discover creators in the broader metro area
- Reduce travel costs by collaborating closer to home
- Build connections in your geographic region
