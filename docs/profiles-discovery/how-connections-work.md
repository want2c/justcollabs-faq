# How do connections work?

Connections on JustCollabs let you build a **professional network** of creators and industry professionals you've worked with or want to collaborate with.

## Sending a connection request

1. Visit a creator's profile from the **Performers** or **Roles** page
2. Tap the **Connect** button on their profile
3. Your request is sent to the other person for review

## Receiving connection requests

1. Go to **Settings > Connections** to see your pending requests
2. Review incoming requests
3. **Accept** to add them to your network, or **Reject** to decline

## Managing your connections

All your connections are managed in **Settings > Connections**, where you can:

- View all your current connections
- See pending requests (sent and received)
- Remove existing connections

## What connections do

- Show that you're an active member of the community
- Help build your professional reputation
- Your connection count is visible on your profile
- Connected users can message each other directly

## Tips for building connections

- **Be genuine**: Connect with people you've actually worked with or want to collaborate with
- **Complete your profile**: A thorough profile makes others more likely to accept your request
- **Be professional**: Write a thoughtful message when reaching out
- **Stay active**: Regularly check your connection requests in Settings
