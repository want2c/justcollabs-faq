# How can I make my profile stand out?

Some tips will help make your profile stand out:

- Use a clear profile photo (use the built-in crop tool in **Settings > Profile** to get the perfect frame), write a short but engaging bio, and link to your top platforms. Mention your content style, collaboration interests, and availability.
- If you are a performer, make sure to fill in as many details as possible from your performer profile.
- Connect your Bluesky account to showcase your work — your Bluesky gallery will be displayed directly on your profile page.
- Add your content platform URLs so visitors can easily find your work.
