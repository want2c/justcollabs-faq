# How does the photo gallery work?

If you've linked your Bluesky account, your profile displays a **photo gallery** showcasing your latest images from Bluesky. This is the best way to show off your work — including NSFW content — directly on your JustCollabs profile. Any images you post to Bluesky will automatically appear in your gallery.

## What does the gallery show?

The gallery pulls up to **10 recent images** from your Bluesky posts. It only includes original posts with images — reposts and replies are excluded. Images are displayed in a responsive grid.

## Lightbox Viewer

Tap any image to open it in a full-screen lightbox viewer. From there you can:

- **Swipe or use arrow buttons** to navigate between images
- **Zoom in** for a closer look
- **Press Escape** or tap outside the image to close

## How do I connect my Bluesky account?

Go to **Settings > Profile** and link your Bluesky handle. Once connected, the gallery will appear automatically on your profile.
