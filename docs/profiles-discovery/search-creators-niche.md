# Can I search for creators in a specific niche or region?

Yes, absolutely! JustCollabs offers a comprehensive filter system to help you find the right collaborators.

## By Region/City

On the **Performers** page (accessible from the bottom navigation bar), you can select any city using the location picker. This can be your home city, a city you're planning to travel to, or any other city you're interested in.

## By Filters

Tap the filter icon on the Performers page to open the full-screen filter overlay. You can filter by:

- **Gender** — narrow by gender identity
- **Age** — set an age range
- **Height** — filter by height range (displayed in your preferred unit from [Preferences](/technical-support/preferences-settings))
- **Weight** — filter by weight range (displayed in your preferred unit from [Preferences](/technical-support/preferences-settings))
- **Body Type** — Toned, Average, Large, Muscular, Slim, or Stocky
- **Position** — Top, Vers Top, Vers, Vers Bottom, Bottom, or Side (only shown when men are viewing men)
- **Ethnicity** — filter by ethnicity
- **Face** — Shows face, No face, Masked, Blurred, or Can discuss
- **Experience** — Beginner, Intermediate, or Professional
- **Verified** — toggle to show only verified creators, only unverified, or all

Each filter section can be toggled on or off independently. The results update as you apply filters.

### Global Filter Toggle

You can enable the **global filter** to keep your filters applied across all cities as you browse. When disabled, filters reset when you change locations.

## Roles Page

For industry professionals (photographers, videographers, editors, etc.), use the **Roles** page from the bottom navigation bar. This page lets you filter by role type and location.

These filters allow you to narrow down your search and find creators who match your specific collaboration needs and preferences.
